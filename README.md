## 🎯 What is goyak-desktop?

goyak-desktop is a free, open-source application that translates spoken language in real time while you play games or use desktop programs. It listens to audio from your computer and instantly converts it to another language, with AI-generated voice that sounds natural. No more waiting for subtitles – hear every conversation as it happens.

Whether you're playing a Japanese RPG, watching a French tutorial, or joining a German voice chat, goyak-desktop breaks down language barriers without slowing you down.

## ✨ Key Features

- **🖥️ Works with any game or app** – Captures system audio, so nothing special is required from your software
- **⚡ Real-time processing** – Translations appear within seconds, with minimal lag
- **🎭 Natural AI voices** – Uses Google's Gemini API for context-aware, human-like speech output
- **🌍 Multiple language pairs** – Translate between dozens of languages, including English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, French, German, and more
- **🔧 Adjustable speed and quality** – Choose faster response or higher accuracy based on your needs
- **🎛️ Easy controls** – Start, pause, and switch languages with simple hotkeys
- **🔒 Privacy-friendly** – Runs locally on your machine; audio is only sent to the translation service when you're actively dubbing
- **💯 Totally free** – No subscriptions, no ads, no hidden costs

Find the goyak-desktop icon on your desktop or in your Start Menu. Double-click it to open the application.

1. When the app opens, you'll see a simple window with language options

To test it, play a game or video with spoken dialogue. You should hear the translated voice shortly after.

## 📝 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does it work with any game?**  
Yes – as long as the game produces audio output, goyak-desktop can capture it. This includes PC games, emulators, streaming apps, video players, and voice chat software.

**Do I need a Google account?**  
No account is required for basic use. However, for unlimited translations or higher quality, you can provide your own Gemini API key in Settings.

**Is there a Mac version?**  
Currently, goyak-desktop is designed for Windows. A Mac version may come in the future if there's enough demand.

**Will it slow down my games?**  
No – the app uses very little CPU. The AI translation happens over the internet, so the heavy lifting is done on Google's servers.
